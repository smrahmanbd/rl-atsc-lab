How it fits the course

This module sits beside the SCATS, SCOOT and Agentic ATSC labs. SCATS and SCOOT are adaptive systems built on designed rules. Here the controller learns its rule. The Agentic ATSC lab then uses a learning agent as one of several tools, with a hands-on Q-learning lab in SUMO.

All numbers in the Foundation lesson come from a small simulated junction with two one-way roads and are illustrative. It has no pedestrians, turning movements or minimum green times.
